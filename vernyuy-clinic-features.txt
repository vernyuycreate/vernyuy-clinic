VERNYUY CLINIC WEBSITE DEMONSTRATION: FEATURE LIST
===================================================
Frontend-only, multi-page demo of a multi-specialty clinic in Buea, Cameroon.
Stack: HTML, CSS, vanilla JavaScript, GSAP + ScrollTrigger (cdnjs). No backend, no build step.
Made by Vernyuy Services. All records, doctors, staff and testimonials are fictional.


1. PAGES (14)
-------------
- index.html               Home
- about.html               About us
- services.html            Services grouped by need
- departments.html         Seven departments (anchors #d-general, #d-pediatrics, #d-womens, #d-dental, ...)
- doctors.html             All doctors, with filtering
- doctor.html?id=N         Individual doctor profile
- team.html                Medical leadership, nursing and support staff
- children.html            Children's care by age group
- patient-information.html What to expect before, during and after a visit (anchors #pi-0 ... #pi-6)
- resources.html           Health articles (resources.html?q=title pre-filters)
- faq.html                 Frequently asked questions with category filter and search
- contact.html             Contact form
- appointment.html         Appointment request form (?doctor=N or ?dept=Name pre-selects)
- admin.html               Admin dashboard demo

Every page has its own header, footer, title and meta description. All links are normal
links, so Back, refresh, bookmarks and sharing work natively.


2. HOME PAGE
------------
- Hero with headline, call-to-action buttons and illustrated artwork (or photo)
- Rotating announcement bar
- Animated counters (departments, example services)
- Department marquee and ECG pulse line under the hero
- "Your health. Your family. Our priority." introduction
- Little patients section linking to children's care
- "One place. Care for the whole family." tiles: children, adults, women, older adults
- Meet our doctors (card preview)
- "Inside the clinic" draggable carousel with Prev/Next buttons and progress bar
- Rotating testimonials with Previous/Next controls
- Health and wellness resources teasers (1 featured + 3 smaller)
- Common questions preview
- Booking call-to-action band
- Vernyuy Services promotion block with WhatsApp links


3. DOCTORS, DEPARTMENTS AND TEAM
--------------------------------
- Doctor cards with specialty, department and short bio
- Buttons: "View profile" and "Request appointment" (pre-selects that doctor)
- Doctor profile page: introduction, sample qualifications, consultation information, department
- Departments page: services list per department, doctors per department, anchor links
- Team page: medical leadership, nursing and pediatric care, support staff, laboratory/other teams


4. CONTENT AND INFORMATION PAGES
--------------------------------
- Services page: example services grouped by category, with who each is for
- Children's care: age-group rows (infants, toddlers, preschool, school-age, adolescents)
- Patient information: seven sections with a chip navigation that scrolls to each
- Resources: article cards, category filter chips, text search, "Read more" expanders
- FAQ: accordion answers, category chips, search box
- About page: story, values and team copy


5. FORMS
--------
Appointment request form (name, email, phone, department, preferred doctor, date, time,
appointment type, optional note) and contact form (name, email, subject, message).
- Client-side validation with inline error messages
- Errors announced to screen readers (aria-live), focus moved to the first problem,
  shake and fade feedback on error
- Doctor list narrows to the chosen department
- Arriving from a doctor or department page pre-fills the form
- Success screens; nothing is sent anywhere and no medical details are collected


6. ADMIN DASHBOARD DEMO (admin.html)
------------------------------------
- Overview: totals (all, pending, confirmed, completed), weekly requests bar chart,
  department breakdown chart, animated figures
- Appointments: search by patient or department, status filter, Confirm / Complete / Cancel actions
- Doctors: bookings per doctor and an Available / Away toggle
- Messages: mark read / unread
- Settings: theme choice (light or dark) and "Reset demo data"


7. THEME
--------
- Opens in the light theme by default, regardless of the device's own setting
- Sun/moon toggle in the header on every page (desktop and mobile)
- Choice remembered across pages (LocalStorage key "cp-theme"), applied before first
  paint so there is no flash
- Smooth colour fade when switching
- Browser theme-color meta updates with the theme
- Admin Settings > Appearance stays in sync with the header button


8. PHOTO LOADING (js/images.js)
-------------------------------
- Photos are picked up automatically from assets/images/ by file name; no code edits
- Extensions checked: .jpg, .webp, .png, .jpeg
- Missing files leave the illustrated placeholder in place, so images can be added gradually
- Photos load lazily as they approach the viewport
- Descriptive alt text added automatically for doctors and team members
- Dark gradient added behind text on photo tiles and carousel slides for legibility
- Supported slots: hero, children feature, family tiles (4), doctors (5), team leads (2),
  nurses (3), departments (7), clinic facilities (6), resources (8), age groups (5, optional),
  about-team (optional), testimonial avatars (3, optional)
- favicon.png replaces the browser icon; social-share.jpg adds an og:image tag


9. MOTION AND ANIMATION (GSAP)
------------------------------
Core motion (main.js and polish.js):
- Hero headline words rise out of a mask
- Staggered scroll reveals for cards and sections
- Hero artwork parallax on scroll
- Magnetic buttons on mouse devices
- Smooth accordion open and close
- Filter results settle in rather than snap
- Mobile "Book an appointment" bar slides in after the hero
- Scroll progress bar and nav shadow on scroll
- Admin figures count up and charts draw in

Added motion layer (js/fx.js), looping:
- Drifting colour blobs behind the hero and page headers
- Radar ring pulsing around the hero artwork
- Floating hero badges that bob out of sync
- Heartbeat (lub-dub) on the logo mark
- Glowing ECG pulse travelling along a line under the hero
- Department-name marquee that slows on hover
- Rising "+" crosses in call-to-action bands
- Light sweep across primary buttons
- Floating department initials on the departments page
- Slow breathing zoom on the hero photo once added

Added motion layer, scroll and pointer:
- Every h2 rises word by word as it scrolls into view
- Photos fade and zoom in, then drift with scroll parallax
- 3D card tilt following the mouse on doctor, resource, family and similar cards

Performance and accessibility of motion: loops pause while off-screen; all animation
is skipped for visitors who prefer reduced motion.


10. ACCESSIBILITY AND USABILITY
-------------------------------
- Skip-to-content link
- Semantic landmarks, labelled form fields, aria-live error messages, aria-invalid states
- aria-expanded on the mobile menu; Escape and outside click close it
- Visible focus styles on interactive elements
- Decorative elements are aria-hidden
- Responsive layout from phone to desktop; safe-area support on notched phones
- Mobile navigation menu and fixed booking bar
- Works from a double-click (file://) as well as on a static host


11. PRIVACY AND DEMO NOTES
--------------------------
- Forms send nothing and collect no medical details; success screens say so
- Only the theme choice is stored (LocalStorage)
- Items marked "to be confirmed" (hours, phone, payments, insurance) must be replaced
  with the real clinic's details before going live


12. FILE STRUCTURE
------------------
index.html, about.html, services.html, departments.html, doctors.html, doctor.html,
team.html, children.html, patient-information.html, resources.html, faq.html,
contact.html, appointment.html, admin.html

Where to edit content (js/main.js): DOCS (doctors), DEPTS (departments), SV (services),
FQ (FAQ), RC (articles), TL/TN/TB/TS (team), AP (admin records), QT (testimonials).
